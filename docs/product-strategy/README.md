## Decision vs implementation

Owner approval means the strategy is the authoritative basis for **future** product/billing/UX/analytics decisions. It is not evidence that features have shipped, Beta is safe, payment processing is authorized or a release has passed external go-live gates.

Change procedure: discuss revised scope, document impact on existing Epics/Issues, obtain explicit owner confirmation, version the strategy and propose changes through **one small PR**, validated by CI and merged by the owner. Do not create competing product epics or override the canonical E1–E22 technical backlog.

For S07, product analytics owner decisions are server-authoritative Trainer/Athlete activation and separate W4 ≥50%, weekly active coached pairs as North Star (independent Athletes tracked separately), versioned typed event dictionary, minimal Beta cohorts (role/platform/activation period, legally supported invite source), no detailed demographic tracking in Beta, controlled optional UX telemetry, small-cohort privacy and signed-off future experiments. Billing metrics stay under existing [#532–535](https://github.com/sauron9521/gym-platform/issues/532) and [E21 #496](https://github.com/sauron9521/gym-platform/issues/496), while general Admin aggregates remain [#296](https://github.com/sauron9521/gym-platform/issues/296) and Governance [#550](https://github.com/sauron9521/gym-platform/issues/550). **No fabricated revenue or paid KPI before verified payment facts and applicable legal/commercial gates.** **No mass implementation backlog changes until S01–S12 are approved**; only owner-approved S07 strategy docs now. Product analytics policy does **not** prove the feature is implemented, or grant Beta/production GO; S08 NOT_STARTED.
